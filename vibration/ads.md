## What we do not collect

To be explicit, the App does not collect, request, or transmit:

- Precise or GPS location. The App declares no location permission.
- Your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or calendar.
- Photos, camera, microphone, or files from your device.
- Your vibration patterns, presets, favourites, or app settings. These are written to local storage on your device and are never uploaded to us. Uninstalling the App deletes them.

The App declares these Android permissions: `VIBRATE` (to drive the vibration motor), `WAKE_LOCK` (to keep the device awake while a pattern plays), `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE` and `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` (to keep playing in the background and show the notification that says so, only if you choose that setting), `INTERNET` (for ads and analytics), and the advertising-identifier permission (`AD_ID`) described below.

## What is collected, by whom, and why

We do not operate a backend. All data described here is collected directly by Google as our service provider, through SDKs embedded in the App.

### 1. Advertising — Google AdMob

To show ads, the AdMob SDK processes:

- **Your device's advertising identifier** (Google Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS) — a resettable ID used to measure ad performance and to select relevant ads. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google serves *limited ads* instead, which do not use it (see *Consent and personalized ads*).
- **Your IP address**, from which an **approximate location** (typically country- or city-level) may be inferred to serve region-appropriate ads and comply with local law. This is not GPS data and does not identify your address.
- **Device and app information** such as device model, operating system version, language, and App version.
- **Ad interaction data** such as which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped.

Google acts as an independent controller for some of this processing. See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [AdMob/AdSense privacy notice](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

### 2. Analytics — Google Analytics for Firebase

The App can send usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google Ireland Ltd., which processes the data on our behalf under Google's data processing terms. We use it, only in aggregate, to see which features are used and where the App has problems.

**When it is on:**

- **In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland, Turkey, South Korea, China and India**, and wherever the App cannot tell where you are (see *How the App tells where consent is required*), it is **off** until you switch it on under *Privacy settings → Help improve the app* and confirm in the dialog that follows.
- **In Canada, Japan and Brazil** it is on by default, and you can switch it off in the same place.
- **Everywhere else** it is on, and the App does not offer the setting.
- Before that decision is made, nothing is collected.

**What it collects while on:**

- A random **app instance ID**, generated by Firebase and stored on your device, so uses of the same installation can be counted together. Your advertising identifier is **not** collected.
- **Usage events** — sessions, first open, App updates, screens opened, and features used: starting and stopping playback, selecting, saving, renaming or deleting a pattern, changing speed, repeat or the keep-running setting, opening the menu, tapping the ad-free upgrade, completed purchases, and the rating prompt being shown. For your own patterns only *that* something happened is recorded, never their names or contents; the names of the built-in presets are recorded.
- **Device and app information** — device model, operating system version, language, App version, and the variant of a small experiment you were assigned to (which of two prompts the App shows).
- **Your IP address**, used by Google to derive your country and not stored.

**What it is not used for:** advertising. The App tells Firebase that none of this data may be used for ads or ad personalization, and Google signals are switched off.

When you switch it off, collection stops and the app instance ID is deleted from your device. See [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Firebase's privacy and security information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

### How the App tells where consent is required

To decide whether usage statistics may start without asking, the App checks on your device: the country of your Google Play or App Store account, the country of your mobile network and SIM card (Android only), your time zone, and your language and region setting. If any of these points to one of the countries above, the strictest rule among them applies; if none of them tells where you are, usage statistics stay off until you switch them on. This check runs entirely on your device; its inputs and result are not sent to us or anyone else. It also uses Google's consent SDK, which asks Google's servers whether consent rules apply in your region.

### 3. Purchases — Google Play Billing

If you buy the ad-free upgrade, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play. **We never receive your payment details.** The App receives confirmation that the purchase succeeded and records a flag in local storage on your device so ads stay off. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 4. Ratings prompt

The App may ask you to rate it. The prompt is shown by the Google Play in-app review flow; any review you write goes to Google Play, not to us, and is governed by Google's terms.

## Consent and personalized ads

The App never shows a consent dialog on its own.

- **In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland** you see Google's *limited ads*: ads selected without your advertising identifier and without storing or reading anything on your device for advertising. Google may still use cookies or local storage to detect invalid traffic and fraud, and uses your IP address to deliver the ad. Personalized ads are not offered there at the moment; if that changes, it will be an option you have to switch on yourself under *Privacy settings*, through a Google-certified consent management platform.
- **Everywhere else** ads may be personalized based on your advertising identifier. You can turn that off in your device settings (see below).

## Your choices and how to opt out

- **Usage statistics** — switch them on or off under *Privacy settings → Help improve the app* in the App's menu, where the App offers it (see *Analytics*).
- **Turn off ad personalization system-wide** — Android: *Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID* (or *Opt out of Ads Personalization*). iOS: *Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking*.
- **Reset your advertising identifier** — this breaks the link to previously collected ad data. Android: *Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID*.
- **Adjust Google's own ad settings** — [myadcenter.google.com](https://myadcenter.google.com).
- **Remove ads entirely** — buy the one-time ad-free upgrade in the App. Once active, the ads SDK is not initialized and no advertising data is sent.
- **Stop everything** — uninstall the App. This ends all collection and deletes all locally stored data.
